I'm Petr Molchanov, an applied AI engineer with a fullstack background. I design multi-agent LLM systems: an orchestrator model plans the work and checks the results, and cheaper subagents do the research in parallel.

I have shipped web products for 8+ years, mostly with React, TypeScript, and Node.js.

## What do I work on?

Since 2023 I have been a Senior Frontend Engineer at ROGII (Houston, TX, remote). I build the React/TypeScript frontend of a cloud platform for well data, and the AI tooling the engineering team uses.

On my own time I build agent systems. The main one is a multi-agent research agent for company due diligence. One run maps a company's subsidiaries and contractors, then tests hypotheses about the company against public sources. A run takes about two hours. The same research by hand takes two days or more. The details are on the [projects page](/projects/).

From 2022 to 2026 I was a contractor for ROKO Labs (New York) on React/TypeScript products for financial data. I cut bundle size by 15%, built a data grid that scrolls 10,000+ rows at 60 fps, and wrote a Python/FastAPI module that monitors AWS storage. Earlier in my career I built e-learning and B2B systems as a fullstack engineer.

## What do I write about?

- Multi-agent systems in practice: architecture, cost, and what breaks.
- How to make agent output trustworthy: verification, confidence, and entity resolution.
- Fullstack engineering for AI products: data, reports, and deployment.
